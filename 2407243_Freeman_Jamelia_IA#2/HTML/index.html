About

Coding Conf is a one-day gathering for students, developers and anyone curious about how software gets built. On October 26, 2026, we’re bringing the community together in Papine, Kingston, for a day of learning, building and connecting. Whether you’re writing your first line of HTML or shipping projects of your own, there’s a place for you here. Registration is free. Reserve your spot, generate your ticket and join us.

What to Expect

  • Short, focused sessions on web development, design and the tools developers use every day.
  • Hands-on time with your laptop. Build something real alongside other attendees and get unstuck together.
  • Meet fellow students and developers, swap ideas and share your GitHub profile with new connections.
  • Leave with fresh ideas, new contacts and the motivation for your next project.
